2. The Business & Technical Challenge

Prior to this engagement, the organization faced compounding operational challenges that threatened business continuity and regulatory compliance.

The healthcare network suffered from failing legacy hardware, slow remote access for doctors and nurses, and high maintenance costs for on-premises storage. A failed prior attempt by a local contractor caused broken permissions and 12 hours of unexpected downtime during a weekend shift. Key Challenges: 1. Migration of 45 Terabytes of highly sensitive electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) across 12 regional hospital facilities. 2. Strict requirement for zero downtime during 24/7 hospital operations and emergency room shifts. 3. Complex permissions mapping involving legacy Active Directory Security Groups to Entra ID (Azure AD). 4. Compliance validation for HIPAA, HITECH, and Joint Commission audits.

3. Architectural Solution & Engineering Execution

Rohit Kumar engineered an end-to-end, multi-stage architectural solution built on Microsoft best practices, Zero-Trust security principles, and high-performance automation.

Rohit Kumar designed a multi-threaded migration engine utilizing Microsoft SPMT and AvePoint Migration Platform, backed by pre-migration remediation scripts that sanitized file paths, repaired broken inheritance, and staged user cutovers in wave cohorts. Architecture & Execution Steps: - Pre-Migration Audit: Executed automated PowerShell PnP inventory scripts to identify long paths, unsupported characters, and stale permissions across 4.2 million files. - Delta Sync Engine: Implemented continuous delta synchronization during off-peak hours, allowing end-users to continue working without interruption. - HIPAA Security Baseline: Configured SharePoint Online tenant settings with strict BAA compliance, disabling external link sharing for ePHI repositories and enforcing MFA via Conditional Access. - Clinical User Training: Provided interactive video walkthroughs and role-based cheat sheets for clinical staff to adopt modern SharePoint libraries seamlessly.

5. Technical FAQ

How is HIPAA compliance guaranteed during SharePoint migration?

We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Microsoft, enforce encryption at rest and in transit, configure Purview DLP rules for ePHI, and disable non-compliant external sharing.

Will end users lose access during the file migration?

No! By leveraging background delta sync technology, users continue accessing their legacy files until a final 15-minute weekend cutover window.

How are long file path errors handled during migration?

Our automated pre-migration scripts scan all directories and automatically truncate paths exceeding 400 characters while generating a mapping log.

Can clinical staff access patient files securely on mobile tablets?

Yes, via managed iOS/Android devices enforcing Intune app protection policies and biometric authentication.
