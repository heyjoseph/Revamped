Nurturing Citizen Developers Without Shadow IT Risks

As organizations embrace low-code automation with Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse, and Power BI, central IT departments face a dual challenge: empowering business units to solve local operational problems quickly while preventing unmanaged "Shadow IT" sprawl, compliance leaks, and orphaned automation flows.

The Microsoft Power Platform Center of Excellence (CoE) Starter Kit provides a comprehensive collection of components and tools designed to help IT administrators gain total visibility into tenant assets, enforce DLP connector boundaries, nurture a community of makers, and streamline Application Lifecycle Management (ALM).

Core Pillars of CoE Setup

  • ✓ Core Inventory Engine: Automated daily sync of all tenant apps, flows, chatbots, and environments.
  • ✓ DLP Connector Policies: Blocking non-business data exfiltration between M365 and external APIs.
  • ✓ Developer Nurture Hub: Welcome emails, maker training paths, and reusable component libraries.
  • ✓ Compliance & Inactivity Workflows: Auto-archiving abandoned flows and unowned apps.

Environment Topology Standard

1. Personal Productivity (Default Env)

Strict DLP policy (standard M365 connectors only). No premium or custom APIs allowed.

2. Developer Sandbox Environments

Isolated developer environments provisioned per maker group for building & testing apps.

3. Production Business Unit Envs

Dedicated Dataverse environments for Finance, HR, Operations with strict RBAC & backup SLAs.

4. CoE Admin Environment

Dedicated admin environment hosting the CoE Starter Kit solutions, dashboards, and audit data.
